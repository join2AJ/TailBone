Meet your specialist

Dr Rajveer Singh

BPT · Fellowship in Orthopaedic Rehabilitation (Apollo Hospital, Chennai)

Exclusive specialist for coccyx and tailbone pain: non-surgical treatment in New Delhi and online.

Dr Rajveer Singh has spent over a decade focused on one problem that many people are told to "just live with": tailbone pain. Patients travel from across India and from the Middle East to see him for Internal Manipulation, a technique few practitioners in the region offer.

Patients describe him as easy to reach by call, SMS, WhatsApp and email, and they say the treatment is quick, respectful and not embarrassing.

Conditions we treat

  • Tailbone pain (coccydynia)
  • Post-pregnancy tailbone pain
  • Subluxed or dislocated coccyx
  • Pain while sitting
  • Tailbone pain after a fall or injury
  • Chronic tailbone discomfort
