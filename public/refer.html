Partner terms

  • Refer only people who have, or may have, tailbone pain, and describe our care honestly. Please don't promise results.
  • Rewards are for new patients only (not people who have already been treated by us), and a patient is linked to the first partner whose ID they use.
  • Rewards for a visit that is cancelled or not attended are withdrawn. We pay what is due once a month and may ask for your PAN for our tax records.
  • You see only each patient's initials and how many visits they've had, never their contact or health details. Patients are told this when they enter your ID.
  • Doctors, hospitals and insurers: please make sure receiving referral rewards is allowed by your professional and regulatory rules. You can also be a partner without rewards; just tell us.
  • Reward amounts may change; changes apply to new rewards only. The clinic may pause any partner account, for example for misleading promotion.
