Doctor at your place

Dr Rajveer can come to you.

For a family, a group of patients, a company or a hospital in another city (or country), Dr Rajveer Singh can travel to examine and treat patients where they are.

How it works

  1. Send a request at least 7 days before the dates you'd like, so the doctor can plan clinic days and travel.
  2. We call or WhatsApp you within 2 working days to agree the dates, the number of patients and the costs.
  3. Once you confirm, the visit is booked. Patients are examined and treated as in the clinic, with follow-up online.

What you cover

  • Treatment fees for each patient
  • Travel both ways (flight or train) and local transport
  • Meals during the visit
  • Stay (a hotel near the patients)

We share an estimate before anything is booked. Nothing is payable until you confirm.
