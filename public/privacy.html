Privacy statement

Last updated: 17 October 2026

What we collect

When you book an appointment we store your name, phone number, optional email, the clinic, date and time you chose, and anything you tell us about your pain. If you complete a follow-up check-in we store your pain score and comments. If you share a story we store what you submit, and we publish only your first name, last initial, city and story after our team reviews it.

This information is kept on the clinic's own server. The site has no accounts for patients, no analytics trackers and no advertising cookies. The only thing saved in your browser is your light or dark theme choice.

If you call, email or message us, we receive the details you choose to share.

How we use it

We use your information only to arrange appointments, provide care and follow up on your treatment. We never sell or rent personal data.

Medical records

Clinical notes and reports are kept confidential and handled in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Third-party services

Embedded maps are provided by Google, chat by WhatsApp (Meta), and Instagram videos by Instagram (Meta), which load only when you tap to play. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email contact@tailbonetreatment.com or call +91 98866 88323.
