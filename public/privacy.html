1. Who is responsible for your data

Tailbone Treatment Clinic (Dr Rajveer Singh) is the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for patients covered by it.

Grievance & privacy contact: Dr Rajveer Singh · contact@tailbonetreatment.com · +91 98866 88323. We reply to privacy requests and grievances within 30 days.

2. What we collect

  • Identity & contact: name, phone/WhatsApp number, optional email.
  • Booking details: clinic, date and time, booking reference, preference for a female clinician.
  • Health information you share (sensitive data): symptoms, pain scores, check-in answers, and the clinician's notes.
  • Payment records: amount, status and Razorpay reference. We never receive card, UPI or bank details.
  • Stories you choose to submit, with your consent to publish.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, time, page) kept for security. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The only items stored in your browser are your light/dark theme choice and, for clinic staff, a sign-in cookie.

4. Who we share it with

Only service providers (Data Processors) who help us run the clinic, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Hosting: Netlify (website) and our application host (Render), which stores the encrypted database.
  • Razorpay: payments and refunds.
  • Meta (WhatsApp): messages you have agreed to receive.
  • Email provider (Resend): notifications to the clinic, if enabled.
  • Google Maps and Instagram: load only when you open a map or tap a video.

International transfers: some providers process data outside India (for example in Singapore, the EU or the USA). DPDP permits this except to countries the Government restricts. For EU patients we rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Patient and clinical records: 3 years after your last visit, then automatically erased.
  • Payment and invoice records: kept de-identified for as long as tax law requires (up to 8 years).
  • Message history: text cleared after 1 year.
  • Security logs: at least 1 year, at most 2 years.
  • Encrypted backups: rotated every 14 days.

6. How we protect it

We use:

  • HTTPS everywhere
  • AES-256 encryption of clinical notes and symptoms in the database, and of all backups
  • individual staff accounts with two-factor sign-in and automatic sign-out
  • account lockout after failed sign-ins
  • a log of who viewed or exported records
  • strict browser security policies
  • daily encrypted backups

If a breach happens, we will inform affected patients and the Data Protection Board of India without delay. Where the GDPR applies, we will also notify the competent EU authority within 72 hours.

7. Your rights

  • Access a summary or copy of your data. Download it yourself from My appointments, or ask us.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure of data we no longer need. Records the law makes us keep, such as payment records, are de-identified instead.
  • Withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect processing already done. WhatsApp updates can be switched off on the My appointments page.
  • Grievance redressal through our contact above. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or to your EU supervisory authority.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (DPDP s.14).
  • GDPR only: data portability, restriction and objection.

8. Make a request

We may contact you to verify your identity before acting. You'll receive a reference number and an answer within 30 days.

9. Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the version date above. For material changes, we'll ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
