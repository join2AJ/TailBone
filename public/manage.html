How to get ready (2 minutes)
  • Find a quiet, private room with the light in front of you, not behind.
  • Charge your phone or laptop, and use Wi-Fi if you can. Earphones help.
  • Prop the phone up so your hands are free; you may be asked to show how you sit and move.
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothes.
  • Write down your questions. If you already have earlier reports, keep them handy; you don't need any new tests.
