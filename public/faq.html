Tailbone pain: frequently asked questions

FAQ

Questions patients often ask.

What is coccydynia?

It's the medical name for pain in and around the coccyx (tailbone). It usually gets worse when sitting, especially when leaning back, and when standing up after sitting.

What is Internal Manipulation, and does it hurt?

It's a hands-on, non-surgical technique that frees up the tight ligaments and muscles attached to the coccyx and restores its alignment and movement. It is quick and done with full consent and privacy. Patients commonly report it's far less uncomfortable than they expected.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your diagnosis, how long you've had the pain and your general health. Some people need very few visits, and others need a short course. We reassess your progress at every visit.

What should I bring to my appointment?

A short note of how and when the pain started, any past injuries or operations, and a list of the medicines you take. Wear comfortable clothing.

Do I need a referral from a doctor?

No. You can book directly with us by phone, WhatsApp or the form above.

Is a female clinician available?

Yes. A female specialist trained by Dr Rajveer Singh is available. Just mention it when you book.

I live outside India. Can you help?

Yes. Many of our patients travel from abroad, including from the Middle East. Start with an online consultation and we'll help you plan the visit.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Open My appointments, enter your appointment number and phone number, and pick a new time or cancel. You can also call or WhatsApp +91 98866 88323.
