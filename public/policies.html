Terms, cancellation & refund policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Applies to bookings made on this website, by phone or on WhatsApp.

The clinic may change fees and rules from time to time; every change is recorded with its date and time. Your booking always follows the rules in force when you made it.

1. Booking

Appointments can be booked online, by phone or on WhatsApp. Each booking receives an appointment number (e.g. TBA-2610-0007), which is your reference, and each patient a permanent patient ID (e.g. TBP-00042). Keep them to manage your booking at My appointments. A booking is confirmed once the clinic confirms it or, where online payment is required, once the payment succeeds.

2. Fees & payment

  • Consultation in New Delhi: ₹800
  • Online video consultation: ₹800
  • Follow-up / review visit within 7 days of a completed visit: ₹500

Online payments are processed securely by Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets). We never see or store your card or bank details. If online payment is not enabled for your booking, the fee is payable at the clinic. Unpaid online bookings are held for a short time and then released automatically.

3. Cancellation by you

You can cancel at My appointments, or by calling or messaging the clinic. The refund depends on how far ahead of the appointment you cancel:

  • 24+ hours before: 100% refund
  • 4+ hours before: 50% refund
  • Less than 4 hours before: 0% refund

The exact amount is shown before you confirm a cancellation online.

4. Rescheduling

Free rescheduling up to 12 hours before, up to 2 time(s) per booking.

After that, please call the clinic; we'll always try to help.

5. Missed appointments

Appointments missed without notice are not refunded.

6. Cancellation or changes by the clinic

If the clinic has to cancel your appointment, you receive a full refund. If the clinic moves your appointment, you'll be notified immediately and can choose the new time, another time, or a full refund.

7. Refund timelines

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to credit them. You'll receive a message when a refund is issued. If you haven't received it after 10 working days, contact us with your booking reference.

8. Medical disclaimer

Information on this website is general and is not a substitute for a medical consultation. Results vary from person to person. In an emergency, contact your nearest hospital.
